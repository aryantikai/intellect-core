Finance & accounting

Bank statements that reconcile themselves

The situation

Three banks, three currencies, three different export formats. Every month the finance team retyped statement lines into the ledger by hand, and every month a handful of entries went in wrong and had to be traced back.

What we built

A parser that reads all three formats, categorises each line against the chart of accounts, flags anything it is not confident about for review, and exports a ledger-ready file. One upload, one review pass, done.

Legal & compliance

A contract archive you can ask questions

The situation

Hundreds of contracts sitting in folders. Finding one clause meant opening files one at a time and hoping somebody remembered which agreement it was in.

What we built

Automatic extraction of parties, dates, values and renewal terms from every contract into a searchable library. Ask for a clause in plain language, get the clause and the document it came from.

Retail, hospitality & trades

The shoebox of receipts, emptied

The situation

Receipts pile up in a drawer until the end of the quarter, then somebody spends days typing them over. Faded ones get guessed at, some never make it in at all, and the VAT that could have been reclaimed quietly disappears.

What we built

Photograph the receipt on the spot. The AI reads the supplier, date, amount, VAT and category, and asks you to confirm before anything is saved. The photo is kept as proof, and the bookkeeper gets one Excel file with a tab per category. Staff can add receipts without seeing the rest of the books.
