What we do

Services built around your workflow.

Six overlapping areas of work. Most projects sit across two or three of them. If none of it quite fits what you have in mind, we still want to hear it, because that is usually where the interesting problems live.

On this page
  • Data automation
  • AI systems
  • Document extraction
  • Platforms & SaaS
  • Integrations
  • Dashboards
01
Workflow Back-office

Data automation

The repeating manual work that eats your team's week. We map the flow, build the system that runs it, and hand it over documented so it keeps running after we leave.

Statements & invoices

Bank statements, supplier invoices and payment files read into the ledger, categorised and flagged where the system is not sure.

Email to tasks

Incoming email triaged, categorised and turned into assigned tasks in the system your team already uses, with the original message attached.

02
AI Assistants

AI systems

AI where it actually earns its keep. Trained or tuned on your own data, kept inside your own environment, and always answering with the source named so nothing is a black box.

WhatsApp & web assistants

Answering questions from your own documents and databases in plain language, with the source attached to every answer.

Decision support

Rules and models that help your team make consistent decisions on repeating cases, without hiding how the answer was reached.

03
OCR Extraction

Document & data extraction

PDFs, scans, photographs of receipts, semi-structured spreadsheets and email attachments turned into clean, structured data your systems can use. No templates to maintain.

Contracts & policies

Parties, dates, clauses, renewal terms and monetary values pulled out into a searchable library you can query in plain language.

Receipts & forms

Photographs and scans turned into structured records for the bookkeeper, with the original image kept as proof.

04
SaaS Full product

Platforms & SaaS

A full working product, built end to end. Operations software for how your business actually runs, or a proper SaaS you can sell as its own thing.

Operations platforms

Work-order systems, service and dispatch platforms, staff and inventory tools tailored to how your business actually operates.

Web apps & SaaS

Full product builds with accounts, billing, permissions and everything else a modern SaaS needs, priced after a real scoping conversation.

05
API Systems

Integrations

Two systems that should talk to each other but do not. We build the bridge, and we build it so that the next change to either side does not break it.

CRM, ERP, payments

Connecting the tools your team already uses so a change in one shows up in the others, without the copy-paste round.

Messaging & notifications

WhatsApp, email and SMS wired into your systems so the right person hears about the right event at the right time, and no earlier.

06
Reporting Metrics

Dashboards

Numbers you can trust, in one place, that a person can actually explain. Built on top of the data pipelines we set up in the automation and integration work above.

Operational dashboards

Live views of what is happening in your business today, so the decisions in this week's meeting are not made on last month's numbers.

Owner reports

Short, honest reports built for the person who signs the cheques: what is up, what is down, and what we are doing about it.
