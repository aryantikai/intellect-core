01
Workflow Back-office

Data automation

The repeating manual work that eats your team's week. We map the flow, build the system that runs it, and hand it over documented so it keeps running after we leave.

Statements & invoices

Bank statements, supplier invoices and payment files read into the ledger, categorised and flagged where the system is not sure.

Email to tasks

Incoming email triaged, categorised and turned into assigned tasks in the system your team already uses, with the original message attached.

Recent projects
Finance Multi-currency statement reconciliation

Three banks, three currencies, three formats. One upload gives a ledger-ready file per period.

Finance & treasury Automatic exchange-rate ingest

Central-bank rates scraped multiple times a day and written into a data lake, so downstream reports never miss a fixing.

SME back-office Receipts-to-ledger via WhatsApp

Photograph a receipt, the AI reads it, the owner taps yes, and a live Excel or ledger picks it up. No shoebox.

02
AI Assistants

AI systems

AI where it actually earns its keep. Trained or tuned on your own data, kept inside your own environment, and always answering with the source named so nothing is a black box.

WhatsApp & web assistants

Answering questions from your own documents and databases in plain language, with the source attached to every answer.

Decision support

Rules and models that help your team make consistent decisions on repeating cases, without hiding how the answer was reached.

Recent projects
Energy · field ops Voice assistant for field workers

Hands-free assistant that answers job questions from internal manuals and logs inspection notes while a technician keeps working.

Enterprise Company chat assistant on internal docs

Staff ask in plain language, get answers grounded only in the organisation's own policies and files, with the source cited.

Safety & HSE AI-assisted incident reporting

A guided reporting flow that turns a short voice note or form into a full, structured incident report for the safety team.

03
OCR Extraction

Document & data extraction

PDFs, scans, photographs of receipts, semi-structured spreadsheets and email attachments turned into clean, structured data your systems can use. No templates to maintain.

Contracts & policies

Parties, dates, clauses, renewal terms and monetary values pulled out into a searchable library you can query in plain language.

Receipts & forms

Photographs and scans turned into structured records for the bookkeeper, with the original image kept as proof.

Recent projects
Legal & compliance Contract metadata & clause search

Parties, dates, renewal terms and clauses extracted from a folder full of PDFs into a library that answers questions in plain language.

Policy library Ask-your-policies RAG search

Company policy documents indexed and made searchable in natural language, with the exact paragraph and document cited on every answer.

Document management PDFs to document-system metadata

Uploaded PDFs are OCR'd, key fields extracted with AI and written back as metadata columns on the source system.

Operations platforms

Web apps & SaaS

Recent projects

API Systems

Integrations

CRM, ERP, payments

Connecting the tools your team already uses so a change in one shows up in the others, without the copy-paste round.

Messaging & notifications

WhatsApp, email and SMS wired into your systems so the right person hears about the right event at the right time, and no earlier.

Recent projects

BI & reporting Report migration to a data-lake stack

Customer messaging WhatsApp Business API integration
