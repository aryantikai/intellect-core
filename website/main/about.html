Who we are

A small team that builds things that stay built.

Intellect Core builds custom software for businesses in Suriname and beyond. A great deal of the work that keeps companies here running is still done by hand, and most of it does not have to be.

We are deliberately small. You talk to the person building your system, not to an account manager who relays it. That is also why we turn work down when we think somebody else is a better fit for it.

Everything we deliver is documented and handed over. If you decide to take it in-house or move to another party a year from now, you can. We would rather earn the next project than hold the last one hostage.

Mission & vision

Why we do this.

Two paragraphs. What we get up for, and where we want to be.

Mission

To take over the manual back-office work that keeps businesses in Suriname slow, so their teams get their weeks back and can spend them on the work that actually grows the company.

Vision

A generation of Surinamese businesses that run on software they own, with the same tools any modern company anywhere has, built here, serviced here, and cheaper because of it.

How we work

Small team, direct contact, weekly progress you can see, everything documented and handed over. If we cannot help, we say so.
