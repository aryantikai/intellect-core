How it works

From raw data to a real answer, in three steps

No dashboards, no SQL, no waiting on an analyst. Point Core Insight at your data and just ask.

01

Connect your data

Upload a spreadsheet or connect a source: Sheets, Excel, CSV, a database or your ERP. Core Insight reads the structure automatically.

02

Ask in plain language

Type a question the way you would ask a colleague. No formulas, no query language, in English or Dutch.

03

Get answers and actions

Core Insight returns a clear answer with the numbers, a chart where it helps, and concrete recommendations you can act on.

What it does

One assistant, every data question

The same engine behind our custom analytics work, packaged so anyone on your team can use it.

Instant analysis

Totals, breakdowns, trends and comparisons, summarised in plain language with the chart that makes it click.

Recommendations & proposals

Not just numbers: Core Insight suggests next steps, drafts proposals and flags what deserves your attention.

Duplicate & error detection

Spots double payments, duplicate records and inconsistencies that quietly cost money, and tells you exactly where.

Anomaly spotting

Outliers, unusual spikes and values that break the pattern get surfaced automatically, before they become a problem.

Trends & forecasts

See how the numbers move over time and where they are heading, so decisions run on direction, not guesswork.

Ask anything, in your language

Questions and answers in plain English or Dutch, with sources you can trace back to the exact rows.

Security & privacy

Your data stays yours

Built privacy-first, the way Intellect Core builds everything: your data is analysed for you and no one else.

Encrypted

Encrypted in transit and at rest on every connection and file.

Not used for training

Your data is never used to train shared models. It answers your questions, nothing more.

You set retention

Keep a live connection or delete the data after each answer. Your call.

Runs in your environment

Can be deployed inside your own systems and accounts, with least-access by default.
