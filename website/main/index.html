Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

Is AI reliable enough for real business data?

That's exactly why we design every system with a human at the wheel and output you can verify — our document AI reports its own confidence per field, and anything uncertain is flagged for review instead of silently guessed. Want proof? The free quickscan runs on your data, so you see the accuracy before you spend anything.

What does something like this cost?

First automations start at $350 fixed. But the better question is what the manual work costs now: use the calculator above — most projects pay for themselves within weeks. We quote fixed prices wherever possible, so there are no surprise hours on an invoice.

How long before we see results?

A first automation is typically live in about a week. Larger systems take 3–6 weeks, with a working demo every week so you see progress from day one — never a black box that "will be ready eventually".

Can our team run it without you afterwards?

Yes — that's the standard. Every project ships with documentation, a walkthrough and a proper handover so it's yours, not a dependency on us. If you'd rather we keep it monitored and improving, care plans start at $150/month, but they're optional.

Our data is sensitive. How do you handle that?

Your data stays in your systems and your accounts — we build inside your environment with least-access principles, and nothing is shared with third parties. We're happy to sign an NDA before the first conversation.

We don't know what to automate first.

Perfect — that's what the free quickscan is for. In 30 minutes we look at where your team's hours actually go, and you get a one-page plan ranking the opportunities by hours saved. Even if you never hire us, you'll know exactly where the leak is.
